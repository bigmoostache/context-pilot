# Write Like a Human

A comprehensive reference for avoiding AI writing tells and producing natural, human-sounding prose. Based on documented patterns from Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing" analysis.

---

## WORDS TO NEVER USE (or use very sparingly)

These words are statistically overrepresented in AI writing. Avoid them:

| Word/Phrase | Use Instead |
| --- | --- |
| Additionally (sentence start) | Also, And, Besides, On top of that |
| Delve / delve into | Explore, look at, dig into, examine |
| Pivotal | Important, key, big |
| Crucial | Important, essential, needed |
| Tapestry (abstract) | Mix, combination, collection |
| Testament (abstract) | Proof, sign, example |
| Underscore (verb) | Show, point out, make clear |
| Vibrant | Lively, active, busy |
| Intricate / intricacies | Complex, detailed, complicated |
| Landscape (abstract) | Scene, field, area, world |
| Meticulous / meticulously | Careful, thorough, precise |
| Foster / fostering | Encourage, support, grow |
| Garner | Get, earn, attract, win |
| Showcase | Show, display, demonstrate |
| Enhance | Improve, boost, strengthen |
| Bolster | Support, strengthen, back up |
| Robust | Strong, solid, sturdy |
| Enduring | Lasting, ongoing, persistent |
| Interplay | Interaction, relationship, connection |
| Align with | Match, fit, agree with |
| Key (adjective, overused) | Main, important, central |
| Valuable | Useful, helpful, worthwhile |
| Boasts (a feature) | Has, includes, offers |
| Highlight (verb, overused) | Show, point out, note |
| Emphasizing | Stressing, showing, noting |
| Groundbreaking | New, original, first |
| Renowned | Famous, well-known, noted |
| Diverse array | Range, variety, mix |
| Nestled | Located, set, sitting |
| In the heart of | In central, in downtown, in |
| Exemplifies | Shows, is an example of |
| Commitment to | Focus on, dedication to |
| Profound | Deep, major, significant |

## SENTENCE & STRUCTURE PATTERNS TO AVOID

### Don't avoid the verb "is"

AI systematically avoids simple copulatives. **Use "is", "are", "has", "was" freely.**

❌ "The building serves as a cultural landmark" ✅ "The building is a cultural landmark"

❌ "The park features a large pond" ✅ "The park has a large pond"

❌ "The organization operates as a nonprofit" ✅ "The organization is a nonprofit"

❌ "This represents a major shift" ✅ "This is a major shift"

### Don't append "-ing" analysis phrases

AI loves to tack on participial phrases that editorialize:

❌ "The law was passed in 2010, reflecting broader societal trends toward inclusivity." ✅ "The law was passed in 2010."

❌ "She won the award, highlighting her contributions to the field." ✅ "She won the award."

### Don't use "not just X, but also Y" constructions

❌ "Not just a musician, but also an activist" ✅ "He was a musician and an activist"

### Don't overuse the rule of three

❌ "innovative, dynamic, and transformative" ❌ "its culture, its history, and its people" ✅ Use one or two descriptors, or vary your list lengths.

### Don't over-explain or editorialize

❌ "Despite its many achievements, the organization faces several challenges, including funding constraints, regulatory hurdles, and competition from larger firms." ✅ State facts without the formulaic setup. Just say what the challenges are if relevant.

## STYLE RULES

### Use sentence case in headings

❌ "Impact of Technology and Digitalization on Modern Society" ✅ "Impact of technology and digitalization on modern society"

### Don't overuse bold

- Don't bold every keyword or important term
- Don't do "key takeaways" with bolded terms
- Bold sparingly, only when truly needed for scanning

### Prefer prose over lists

❌ Numbered list with bold inline headers:

> 1. **Cultural Significance:** The festival represents...
> 2. **Economic Impact:** Tourism revenues...

✅ Write it as flowing paragraphs.

### Use em dashes sparingly

AI overuses em dashes (—), especially with spaces. Prefer:

- Commas for minor asides
- Parentheses for supplementary info
- Colons for explanations
- Period + new sentence for separate thoughts

### No emoji as formatting

❌ 🧠 Key Insight: ... ❌ 📌 Important: ... ✅ Just write the text.

### Don't create small tables for simple information

If the info can be a sentence or two, write it as prose.

### Use straight quotes

Use "these" not "these". Use it's not it's.

## VOCABULARY: WHAT TO DO (signs of human writing)

### Use simple, common words

| AI prefers | Humans say |
| --- | --- |
| authored | wrote |
| relocated | moved |
| utilized | used |
| attempted | tried |
| commenced | started, began |
| facilitated | helped |
| implemented | set up, did, ran |
| passed away | died |
| prior to | before |
| subsequent to | after |
| in proximity to | near |
| at this juncture | now |

### Use hedging and qualifiers freely

Humans naturally hedge. AI sounds too confident and definitive:

- "very", "quite", "pretty (informal)"
- "perhaps", "maybe", "probably"
- "tends to", "often", "usually"
- "I think", "it seems like"
- "sort of", "kind of" (informal)

### Use wordy human constructions

These "inefficient" phrases are actually markers of natural writing:

- "as a result of" (not just "because")
- "in order to" (not just "to")
- "all of the" (not just "all the")
- "the fact that"
- "a part of"
- "in terms of"
- "a lot of" (instead of "numerous")

### Use superlatives and strong opinions

❌ (AI hedges via emptiness) "It played a significant role..." ✅ (Human commits) "It was one of the best...", "the first to...", "the only one that..."

### Repeat words naturally

Don't strain to find synonyms. If "said" is the right word, use "said" three times — don't alternate between "stated", "remarked", "noted", "expressed".

## COMMUNICATION PATTERNS TO AVOID

- ❌ "I hope this helps!"
- ❌ "Certainly!" / "Of course!" / "Absolutely!"
- ❌ "You're absolutely right!"
- ❌ "Would you like me to..."
- ❌ "Is there anything else I can help with?"
- ❌ "Let me know if you need..."
- ❌ "Here is a detailed breakdown of..."
- ❌ "Great question!"
- ❌ "That's a really interesting point"
- ❌ "more nuanced understanding"

---

## META & STRUCTURAL TELLS TO AVOID

- ❌ Don't over-explain your changes or reasoning in exhaustive detail
- ❌ Don't mention what you "ensured" or "avoided"
- ❌ Don't write "About Me / My Interests / Let's Connect!" style bios
- ❌ Don't include knowledge-cutoff disclaimers ("as of 2024", "information may be limited")
- ❌ Don't leave placeholder brackets: \[describe...\], \[Your Name\], \[link\]
- ❌ Don't add formulaic "challenges and future outlook" sections
- ❌ Don't treat topics as more significant than they are
- ❌ Don't start paragraphs with the subject's full name + "is a..." definition format

## QUICK SELF-CHECK

Before finalizing any text, ask:

1. **Would a tired human write it this way?** If it sounds too polished, too structured, too perfectly balanced — simplify.
2. **Am I avoiding "is"?** If you catch yourself writing "serves as", "stands as", "functions as" — just say "is".
3. **Am I using the AI vocabulary list?** Ctrl+F for: delve, tapestry, landscape, vibrant, foster, underscore, pivotal, crucial, testament, meticulous, robust, garner, showcase, enhance, bolster.
4. **Are my lists too neat?** Real writing is messier. Not everything needs a bullet point or a header.
5. **Am I editorializing?** Cut any "-ing" phrase at the end of a sentence that interprets significance.
6. **Is this word the simple one?** "Use" not "utilize". "Start" not "commence". "Help" not "facilitate".
7. **Does every paragraph start differently?** Varying sentence openings is good, but don't strain for it unnaturally.
